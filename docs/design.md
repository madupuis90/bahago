# Summary

Bahago (need to be changed) is a game inspired by Bahagon. It is a real time strategy game where you manage a kingdom, gather resouce, construct structures, train armies and fight with your neighbords.

# Mechanics

## Population

Population is the base multiplier for resource gathering, construction speed, etc.

Percentage of population can be assigned to:
- Worker - Enables construction, modify construction speed, population rate
- Woodcutter - Gathers wood
- Miner - Gathers minerals
- Scholar - Gathers knowledge
- Clergy - Gathers devotion
- Farmers - Gathers food
- Disciple - Gathers mana, Prepare spells

## Resources

There are material resources: 
- Wood
- Minerals
- Food - Upkeep of army

There are meta-resource:
- Mana/aether/arcane - Can be spent for spells with rituals, upkeep summun
- Devotion/faith/grace - Boosts worshiper units, triggers miracle, sustain prayers

- Knowledge - Only goes up, unlocks technologies

## Unit Attributes

- Worshipper - boosted by devotion
- Appartion - does not require food
- Pacifism - can't attack
- Raiders - can't block
- Flying - bonus vs melee
- Archer - bonus vs Flying 20%
- Melee - no bonus
- Siege Engine - survives until the end of the round
- ghoulish - take 50% less damage
- deathtouch - 50% more damage to non-undead
- enrage - 30% more power when outnumbered
- shields - 40% less damage from archer
- Gluttony - 30% more food consumed
